# The first season: the story

Northgate End, the suburb outside Cornhaven's north gate, Lady Day to Midsummer 1591. Written by
Claude and reviewed by Ed (17 Nov 2026 pivot to an authored social mystery: fixed town, written
cast, unnamed crowd, simulation underneath). Every line lives in `src/content/suburb/`;
`npx tsx tools/lines.ts` prints them all.

## The premise

You are Jaime Varro, 26, sent home from abroad by the Order of the Hand. Your mother Isobel was
master of the old Lodge of the Hand, which met in the cellar of her house until the Watch raided it
on Lady Day eleven years ago. Aldous Wexville and Marta Quill were hanged at the gate; Isobel fled.
Someone sold the lodge's night. The house is yours now, with weavers (the Cornstows) upstairs.

## The spine (main goals, `GOALS` in `plot.ts`)

1. Read the Order's letter. 2. Go down to the cellar. 3. Open your mother's chest: the cipher
(moon, hand, rose, key) is drawn in her prayer book, which Father Anselm kept and gives you at two
hearts once you say who you are. The chest holds the minute book, her signet, the banner and coin.
4. Furnish the hall (a table, two benches, a candle stand from Joan Wright). 5. Win someone to
the Hand. 6. Swear them below after 19:00. 7. Hold the first meeting. 8. Learn eight of the
twelve councillors' names. 9. Find who sold the old lodge.

## The mystery: who sold the lodge?

The minute book names "R.H.": Rufus Hale of the Bell, who owed Pyke fourteen ducats, refused
Aldous, and told Pyke the night. Pyke sold it to Captain (now Warden) Crane and forgave the debt.
Clues come from Grete (free ale every Lady Day), Moll, Hester (a page of Pyke's ledger), Marta's
hidden letter (in the Quill house, key from Agnes), Anselm and others. With the minute book and
four clues, talk to Rufus after 22:00 at the Bell: show the letter, the ledger, Moll's word, or
bluff (works with six clues). Then choose: he swears to the Hand, serves for fear of Wymarc, is
exposed to Wymarc and leaves, or keeps his secret and owes you. Pyke and Crane are for later years.

## Recruits

Ness Cornstow (wants letters: a primer, then three evening lessons), Wymarc Wexville (show him
the minute book), Agnes Fell (bury Marta in holy ground: Anselm's blessing, a shovel, at night),
Tobin Marsh (pay off his debt to Pyke), Hal Brenner (wants his mother well and to stop
stealing), and Rufus himself if you ask him to pay back what he sold.

## Side errands

Clear the fallen end of the hall, search the Quill house, bring Marta home, open the old way
under the garden (lowers the lodge's exposure).

## Rules kept from the simulation

Clock and 28-day season, written routines, trust (hearts), exposure, facts learned with their
source, the Book, rent and keep, the council ladder (7 of 12 seats, then mayor) as the long goal.
Confrontations are rare and scripted (Rufus is the season's one); there is no combat.

## The season letter

On day 28 the Order judges the season: members sworn, councillors known, how the betrayal was
settled, Marta's burial, and your exposure.
